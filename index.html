About Sylqora AI

Built to help you understand, not just answer.

Sylqora AI is a virtual, interactive study tool designed to act as an AI-powered tutor for struggling students. It focuses on simulating the experience of a conversational "study buddy" rather than a strict classroom teacher. It focuses not on giving you block of answers but rather on helping you understand the topics

Simple Explanations

Complex topics converted to clear, undrstandable steps.

Interactive Learning

Engage with quizzes, flashcards, and follow-up questions.

Your Material

Features

Here are some of the features we offer:

Ask & Learn

You can ask Sylqora AI any question regarding your studies, any complicated topic and Sylqora will give you an explanation using simple wording and examples.

Quiz me

Sylqora will generate a Quiz for any topic you want, check your answers and explain your mistakes.

Upload and study

Upload your notes and Sylqora can summarize them and help you ask follow up questions regarding the material.

Flashcards

Just name the topic and Sylqora will generate flashcards for you so you can revise interactively.
